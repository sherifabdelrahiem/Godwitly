# Crash course Lessons are Arabic-only at first

`AGENTS.md` requires every piece of user-facing text in both English and Arabic. The PO
decided on 2026-10-30 that the Crash course is a deliberate exception: its Lessons are
written in Egyptian Arabic, for the beginners it is aimed at, with technical names (Terminal,
Plan mode, commands) kept in English. The site's shared parts, such as the header, footer and
buttons, stay bilingual and use the site's Modern Standard Arabic. With the language switcher
on English, visitors see the English title, a short English summary and "Available in
Arabic". An English version is later work.

## Considered options

- **Fully bilingual from the start:** matches the rest of the site, but doubles the writing
  and every later update, for an audience the course is not aimed at.
- **Machine-translated English:** rejected; a beginner following wrong or stilted steps alone
  is worse than no English version.

## Consequences

- Do not "fix" missing English Lesson text by translating it; English is its own approved
  piece of work.
- The project checks for translation parity must allow the Lessons' Arabic-only text.
