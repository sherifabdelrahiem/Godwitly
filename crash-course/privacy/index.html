Privacy: Godwitly accounts

Draft. This wording is not final and has not been approved. Parts marked as planned are not built yet.

This page explains what happens to your information when you sign in to a Godwitly Crash course with Google.

Who runs this

Godwitly, by BCM Hub, runs this service. [Legal name, address and contact to be added by the Project owner.]

What we keep

Your Google name, email address and picture, held by our sign-in service. Planned, not built yet: the Crash courses you enrol in and the checklist items you tick. The date you last used your account, and the version of this notice you saw when your account was created. We do not copy your Google details anywhere else.

Why we keep it

To sign you in, to let you enrol in a Crash course, to save your progress, and to let you open Lessons that need enrolment.

Legal basis

Your rights

[Your rights to see, correct and delete your information, and how to use them, to be written with counsel.] Planned: you will be able to delete your Godwitly account yourself. This is not available yet.

Who can see your records

The Project owner can see account records as the administrator of the service. The Course report shows only totals, never an individual's records.

Services that hold your data, and where

Supabase holds accounts, in Frankfurt, Germany. Google signs you in. Cloudflare hosts this website and keeps request logs. [How long Google, Supabase and Cloudflare keep their own logs to be added.]

How long we keep it

Planned, not built yet: an account that has not been used for 24 months will be deleted, within 30 days of becoming eligible. If the service is paused, it will be restored and cleaned up within 30 days of the pause notice. When you delete your account, your records will be removed.

Anonymous totals

Planned, not built yet: when an account is deleted, we will keep anonymous counts of enrolments and completed Crash courses, with nothing that identifies you. Our sign-in and hosting providers keep their own logs for their own periods.
