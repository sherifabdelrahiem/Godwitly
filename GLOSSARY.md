# Godwitly

Godwitly is a platform for learning, engagement and organisational preparedness, offered as
three products. Before launch, its public face is a bilingual (English/Arabic) landing page
that collects launch sign-ups.

## Language

### Platform and products

**Godwitly**:
The single platform an organisation uses; Learn, Engage and Resilience are parts of it, not
separate offerings. Written in Latin letters in Arabic too.
_Avoid_: suite, app, tool

**Product**:
One of Godwitly's three parts: Learn, Engage or Resilience.
Arabic: منتج
_Avoid_: solution, module, app; Arabic: حل، حلول

**Learn**:
The product for structured learning programmes that end in a final exam.
Arabic: تعلُّم (write "منتج تعلُّم" where it could be read as the ordinary word)

**Engage**:
The product that makes training sessions, workshops and events interactive.
Arabic: تفاعُل (write "منتج تفاعُل" where it could be read as the ordinary word)

**Resilience**:
The product that prepares organisations for disruption through scenario-based exercises.
Only ever a product name.
Arabic: مرونة (write "منتج مرونة" where it could be read as the ordinary word)
_Avoid_: resilience or resilient as everyday words; use Preparedness

### People

**Organisation**:
The customer that uses Godwitly. It holds the maturity score and works towards Preparedness.
Arabic: المؤسسة
_Avoid_: client, company, tenant, account

**Training manager**:
The person who acts for an Organisation: sets up programmes, sessions and exercises, and
reviews their results. May also be a Facilitator.
_Avoid_: admin

**Facilitator**:
The person who runs a live Engage session or Resilience exercise while it happens.
_Avoid_: host, trainer, moderator

**Learner**:
A person enrolled in a Learn programme. Readiness belongs to them.
Arabic: متعلّم
_Avoid_: user, trainee, student

**Participant**:
A person taking part in an Engage session or a Resilience exercise. The same person can be a
Learner elsewhere; both are roles, not people.
Arabic: مشارك
_Avoid_: user, attendee, audience member

**Team**:
A named group within an Organisation that takes a Resilience exercise together. Used only in
Resilience.
Arabic: فريق

**Team lead**:
The Participant who leads a Team.
Arabic: قائد الفريق

### Learn

**Programme**:
A structured course a Learner enrols in, made of Domains and ending in a Final exam.
Arabic: برنامج
_Avoid_: learning track, track, course

**Study plan**:
One Learner's personal path through a Programme; it is what shows a percentage complete.
Arabic: خطة دراسة

**Domain**:
One subject area of a Programme; Practice and Readiness are tracked per Domain.
Arabic: مجال
_Avoid_: module, unit, chapter, topic

**Baseline assessment**:
The assessment a Learner takes once at the start of a Programme to set up their Practice.
Arabic: التقييم المبدئي

**Practice**:
Personalised question sets a Learner works through between the Baseline assessment and the
Final exam.
Arabic: الممارسة
_Avoid_: quiz, test; Arabic: التدريب

**Final exam**:
The assessment that ends a Programme.
Arabic: الامتحان النهائي
_Avoid_: final examination, test, quiz; Arabic: الاختبار النهائي

### Engage

**Session**:
One live, interactive Engage occasion run by a Facilitator. The customer's own occasion (a
training session, workshop or event) is not a Godwitly term.
Arabic: جلسة
_Avoid_: event, workshop, meeting

**Live quiz**:
A set of questions Participants answer in real time during a Session. "Quiz" is used only in
Engage.
Arabic: اختبار مباشر

### Resilience

**Exercise**:
One session in which a Team works through a Scenario and makes decisions together.
Arabic: تمرين
_Avoid_: simulation, drill, "tabletop" as a noun on its own

**Scenario**:
The story of a disruption that an Exercise is built on.
Arabic: سيناريو

**Inject**:
A new development released to the Team partway through an Exercise.
Arabic: حدث مستجد

**Maturity assessment**:
The organisation-wide evaluation that produces the Maturity score. Exercises contribute
evidence to it; no single Exercise produces it.
Arabic: تقييم مستوى النضج

### Outcomes

**Preparedness**:
The overall outcome Godwitly helps an organisation reach. A general aim, not a score.
Arabic: الاستعداد المؤسسي
_Avoid_: resilience, readiness; Arabic: الجاهزية

**Readiness**:
A learner's measured progress towards passing a Learn final exam.
Arabic: الجاهزية
_Avoid_: preparedness

**Maturity score**:
An organisation's rating from a Resilience maturity assessment, such as 3.32. It belongs to
the organisation, not to a single team.
Arabic: درجة النضج

### Launch

**Launch**:
The single moment the whole Godwitly platform becomes publicly available. Products do not
launch separately.
Arabic: الإطلاق

**Launch sign-up**:
One person's request, made through the landing page, to be told when Godwitly launches.
Called "sign-up" for short. The person behind it has no separate term.
Arabic: طلب إشعار بالإطلاق
_Avoid_: waitlist entry, registration, lead, subscriber, contact, contact form, "Contact us";
Arabic: التسجيل

**Launch notification**:
The message sent at launch to everyone with a launch sign-up; the only contact they agreed to.
Arabic: إشعار الإطلاق

**Sign-up alert**:
The internal email the Project owner receives for each new launch sign-up. It carries no
personal data.
_Avoid_: notification, notify email

### Crash course

**Crash course**:
The Claude Crash Course for Beginners: a free, self-paced Godwitly guide that teaches
complete beginners to use Claude and Claude Code. It is not a Learn Programme: there is no
enrolment, tracking or Final exam.
Arabic: دليل كلود للمبتدئين (the agreed Arabic title, not a word-for-word translation)
_Avoid_: Programme, course on its own, training; Arabic: كورس، برنامج

**Crash courses**:
The site's section for Crash courses; the header, mobile menu and footer link to it. Until
a second Crash course exists, the link opens the Claude Crash Course for Beginners hub.
Arabic: الدورات المكثّفة (the site's Modern Standard Arabic; not the title of a single Crash
course)
_Avoid_: Courses, Programmes, Learn (a product name); Arabic: كورسات، برامج

**Lesson**:
One part of the Crash course with a single outcome the Course taker can check. Lessons are
numbered from 1 and are not tied to days.
Arabic: درس
_Avoid_: Domain, module, day, unit

**Section**:
One headed part inside a Lesson, such as "Chat vs Code". Lessons are not grouped above the
Lesson level.
Arabic: جزء من الدرس
_Avoid_: chapter (a Learn Domain's word to avoid), part, step; Arabic: فصل

**Self-check**:
A few questions at the end of a Lesson that a Course taker answers to check their own
understanding. It has no score and counts for nothing.
Arabic: اختبر نفسك
_Avoid_: quiz, test, exam, Practice; Arabic: اختبار، امتحان

**Course taker**:
A person working through the Crash course. Used in docs and specs only; the course pages
address them as "إنت".
_Avoid_: Learner (reserved for Learn), student, user, trainee

**Sample business**:
The fictional business, كافيه المرسى (Al-Marsa Café) in Port Said, whose facts file every
Crash course example builds on. A Course taker may use their own business instead, with
permission.
_Avoid_: demo, the bakery

**Resources**:
Optional files that go with one Lesson, which the Course taker downloads and uses while
working, such as the Sample business facts file or a template. A Lesson may have none.
Arabic: المصادر
_Avoid_: annex, training material, attachments; Arabic: ملحق، مرفقات

**References**:
The official pages a Lesson's facts and setup steps come from, linked so a Course taker can
check them when something looks different.
Arabic: المراجع
_Avoid_: sources; Arabic: المصادر (that word means Resources)

**Cheatsheet**:
An optional one-page summary at the end of a Lesson, for looking things up while working:
its key terms, the prompts and commands to copy, and its main warnings. It is part of the
Lesson, not a Resource.
Arabic: برشامة
_Avoid_: summary, reference card; Arabic: ملخّص، ورقة مختصرة

### Access to sign-ups

**Project owner**:
The one person who runs the landing page and decides who may see Launch sign-ups.
Arabic: صاحب المشروع
_Avoid_: admin, superuser

**Sign-up viewer**:
A person the Project owner has allowed to see Launch sign-ups and their figures. Viewing is
all they can do.
Arabic: مُطّلع على طلبات الإشعار
_Avoid_: admin, team member, operator

**Sign-up dashboard**:
The private page where the Project owner and Sign-up viewers see Launch sign-ups and their
figures.
Arabic: لوحة طلبات الإشعار
_Avoid_: admin panel, back office, CRM
